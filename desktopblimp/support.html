Desktop Blimp for iPhone Duo

Support

Desktop Blimp turns your iPhone Duo into a small LED message sign. Type a message on the inner screen, press GO and fold the phone into a tent. The outer screen then shows your message to the room in glowing dots, like a deli sign.

Requires iPhone Duo

Desktop Blimp uses both of iPhone Duo's screens and its hinge. On any other iPhone, it shows a “Made for iPhone Duo” notice and nothing else. It isn't available for iPad.

Getting started

  1. Open your iPhone Duo and launch Desktop Blimp. The writer appears on the inner screen.
  2. Type your message. The live preview shows exactly what the board will show.
  3. Choose a style, colour, speed or effect if you like.
  4. Press GO, then fold the phone into a tent. Your message appears on the outer screen.
  5. Press STOP to freeze the board on the current message.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I only see “Made for iPhone Duo”?

Your iPhone doesn't have a hinge and an outer display, so Desktop Blimp can't run on it. This is expected.

The message isn't showing on the outer screen.

Make sure you pressed GO, then fold the phone into a tent so the outer screen faces out. If the board shows a clock, no message has been sent yet.

Will the screen stay on?

Yes, while the board is showing in tent pose and Desktop Blimp is open, for up to 8 hours by default. You can change this in Settings > Desktop Blimp > Keep Outer Screen On (1, 2, 4 or 8 hours, or Always). After that, or as soon as you close the phone or leave the app, normal Auto-Lock applies. In Low Power Mode the board animates more gently and without glow. For long sessions, we recommend keeping the phone on a charger.

Why does the board look dimmer at night?

Desktop Blimp dims the board overnight, from 10 pm to 6 am, so it's easier on the eyes.

Does the board move slightly?

Yes. A gentle pixel shift moves the content a tiny amount from time to time to help protect the display.

How long can a message be?

Up to 120 characters. If it won't all fit, the writer tells you the board is full.

Can I save messages?

Yes. There are ten message slots. Tap the slot number to switch between them.

What styles and effects are there?

Three styles: Deli (scrolling LED dots), Board (still text sized to fill the screen) and Modern (clean type). Three colours: White, Silver and Soft. Three speeds: Slow, Medium and Fast. You can also add dot-art emoji and one-shot effects such as fireworks, confetti, hearts, sparkles, snow and shake, and choose whether the message loops or plays once.

Does Desktop Blimp collect my data?

No. It has no account, no network connection, no analytics and no ads. Your messages stay on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

How do I reset everything?

Delete and reinstall the app. This removes all saved messages and settings.
