Desktop Blimp for iPhone Duo

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Short version: Desktop Blimp collects no data about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Who we are

Desktop Blimp is developed by JP Corporate Solutions Pty Ltd (ACN 675 702 431), Australia, and published on the App Store under the seller name Eucalyptus Dogfight. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean JP Corporate Solutions Pty Ltd.

What we collect

Nothing. Desktop Blimp does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information or usage data. We don't receive the messages you type, and we have no way to see them.

What stays on your iPhone

To remember your work between launches, the app saves its settings on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). This includes:

  • your saved message slots and the text in them
  • your chosen style, colour, speed, effect and loop setting
  • which message is on the board, and your Keep Outer Screen On setting

This information never leaves your iPhone through Desktop Blimp. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer device backups, which are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No network, analytics or tracking

  • The app makes no network connections.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or other third-party SDKs.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not use the advertising identifier.
  • It asks for no permissions: no location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or notifications.

Apple

When you download or buy the app, Apple processes that transaction under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share diagnostics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. These contain no personal information we can link to you.

Children

Desktop Blimp collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If the app ever began to collect data, we would update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version was released.
